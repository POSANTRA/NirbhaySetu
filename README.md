
# NirbhaySetu

### Cyber Crime Reporting & Safety Guide

NirbhaySetu is a multilingual cyber-safety guide designed to help people understand cybercrime, take the right immediate action, preserve important evidence, and find the appropriate reporting channel.

## About

Cybercrime can affect anyone, and many people are unsure about what to do immediately after an incident.

NirbhaySetu provides simple, situation-based guidance in:

- English
- Marathi
- Hindi

Instead of requiring users to understand technical cybercrime terminology, the platform starts with a simple question:

> **What happened to you?**

Users can then select a situation and receive short, practical guidance.

## Key Features

- Situation-based cybercrime guidance
- English, Marathi and Hindi support
- Financial fraud and 1930 guidance
- Cybercrime reporting information
- Evidence preservation guidance
- Lost/stolen mobile guidance
- Find My Device / Find My iPhone guidance
- CEIR information
- Emergency 112 guidance
- Accessibility-friendly text sizing
- Mobile-responsive interface

## Situations Covered

- UPI / Financial Fraud
- OTP / Fake Calls
- Suspicious Links
- Social Media Hacking
- Online Harassment
- Fake Profiles / Identity Theft
- Online Shopping Fraud
- Job Scams
- Lost or Stolen Mobile

## Project Background

NirbhaySetu was developed as part of a field project on:

**Cyber Crime Reporting Guide in Local Language**

The project involved field interaction with the Cyber Cell and an awareness survey to understand common reporting and cyber-safety gaps.
